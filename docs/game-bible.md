## 3. The concept

> **MISSION X** — *The whole expo is the map. The X is real. Find it.*

The MIHAS floor becomes an **orbital trade station** floating above a teal planet — your backdrop's planet, your banner's starfield. Every exhibitor booth is a **station**. The halls are **sectors**. Far out at the western edge, under a column of golden light and a giant rotating blue-and-yellow **X**, is the **Launch Pad**: booth 8H18B.

The floor plan stays **accurate to the metre** — that is the utility and the credibility. The *skin* is your universe.

### Why the name works
- **X** = lean**.x** digital, ne**x**ova, the giant X on your banner.
- **X marks the spot** = a treasure hunt everyone already understands, in any language.
- **Mission** = astronaut fiction, and it ends in a real place.
- It contains no organiser trademark. Line underneath: *"An expo quest by Lean X Digital. Unofficial — not affiliated with MATRADE or MIHAS."*

### Taglines (from your own banners)
- "Find the X. Take your next digital leap."
- "Think bigger. Grow faster. Start at 8H18B."

## 5. Player journey

### 5.1 Arrival (0–60 s)
Tap the ad → 3–5 s load → the camera drops from orbit through the stars onto the station, sweeps the full length of the floor from the main entrance, and lands on the golden beam at the far western edge. Title: **FIND THE X.** Pick your crew member. You're walking.

No registration yet. No app. Portrait, one thumb.

### 5.2 Mission 01 — Find the X
Two routes, chosen by the player:

| Route | Start | Length | Design purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Short** | Hall 8 entrance | 37 m | Instant gratification; for on-site players who want directions |
| **Epic** | Main entrance (Hall 5 registration) | 228 m, passes ~50 stations | The scenic route — crosses Halls 6, 7, 8; every station passed is a potential stamp and an exhibitor impression |

A pulsing breadcrumb trail leads the way. On arrival: the crew greets you, confetti in brand colours, and the NPC says: *"Every business on this station needs a digital front door. Here's yours — free."*

**Claim → short form → your digital business card is built live on nexova** (`yourname.nexova.my`) — with vCard, QR, WhatsApp button. *The reward is the product demo.* Twelve seconds, on their own phone, with their own name on it.

### 5.3 The real-world finish
The card comes with a **Golden Ticket** (signed, single-use QR). *"Bring this to the real 8H18B."* Your crew scans it at the booth → the player's phone erupts: **MISSION COMPLETE, +500 XP**, physical reward unlocked (suggested: NFC tap-card linked to their new nexova page — the digital card made physical).

### 5.4 Missions that follow — structured on your own funnel

Your A-board already says it: **BRAND → MARKET → SELL.** That is the campaign structure.

| Chapter | Missions | Teaches / sells |
|---|---|---|
| **BRAND** | *First Light* — bring your own card online. *Signal Check* — visit 5 stations and rate their "signal" (does the booth have a QR? a website?). | Awareness of digital presence |
| **MARKET** | *Pavilion Passport* — stamp 8 country pavilions (Korea, Japan, Indonesia, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Philippines, Saudi, China). *Sector Sprint* — timed run touching one station in each of 5 sectors; **skill-ranked leaderboard**. | Reach, traffic |
| **SELL** | *The Closer* — a 60-second funnel-builder mini-game: drag Ad → Landing Page → WhatsApp → Payment in the right order before the timer ends. *Hidden X* — 10 small X tokens hidden around the station. | Your actual service, as play |
| **DAILY DROP** | One new mission each show day; leaderboard resets at 17:00. | Return visits |

### 5.5 Exhibitor mode — "Bring your station online"
(v1's "Claim Your Booth", now in-fiction.)

Every unclaimed booth is a **dark station**. An exhibitor finds their own booth and taps **"This is my station."** They register → the station **powers up**: lights, a beam, their brand colour, their one-line offer, their link. They get a ready-made share card and a 10-second clip: *"Our station is online — find us at 3F09 on Mission X."*

- Online stations become **stamp stops**, so claiming earns real traffic.
- The station page **is a nexova page**. The upsell path is already built.
- The booth's big screen shows the station map filling with light over four days. **That visual — the floor slowly coming online — is your hero film.**

**Each claim = a named decision-maker at a business that just told you it wants digital visibility.**

### 5.6 AI guide — "Ask X"
The floating astronaut answers questions in BM or English: *"Who sells halal gelatin?" "Where is the Korea pavilion?" "Which booths do packaging?"* — then draws the trail there. Built on Claude with the exhibitor directory as its only knowledge source, so it cannot invent exhibitors. This is the feature that makes the press write about it, and it is on-message for an AI-native company.

### 5.7 Presence
Other players appear as small astronauts with their chosen jacket colour. No free-text chat (moderation and brand risk at a government-hosted halal trade show) — **emotes and the smile visor only.** The booth screen shows all of them as moving dots on the miniature.

## 6. Art direction

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **World** | Miniature orbital platform. Deep navy space, teal planet below, soft starfield. Floor: dark teal with a faint 3 m grid — it reads as "blueprint" and as "space station deck". |
| **Palette** | Straight from your kit: navy `#041A2C`, deep teal `#0B5F86`, brand teal `#17B6D6`, cyan glow `#6FE3FF`, **accent yellow `#FFC629`**, white. Yellow is reserved for *the X, the trail, and rewards* — nothing else. |
| **Stations** | Rounded low-poly modules, colour by sector, matte. Dark when unclaimed, glowing when online. Names on illuminated fascia. |
| **Landmarks** | Café & Visitor Lounge, Wellness Corner, MIHAS Corner stage, Kitchen, Media Center, Photo Booth, Live Box, Speaker Lounge, Bernama Studio, Merchandise — each a distinct silhouette, because people navigate by landmarks, not booth numbers. |
| **The Launch Pad** | The only fully detailed booth: your real backdrop on the back wall, the counter, the screen, the crew on beanbags. Golden light column visible from anywhere on Level 2. Rotating blue/yellow X above it. |
| **Characters** | The four astronauts, ~2 k triangles each, stylised proportions (big helmet). Walk, idle, wave, celebrate, sit-on-beanbag. |
| **Type** | Geometric sans to match your lockup (Urbanist / Gilroy class). White + yellow keyword, as in "Building **Brands** Driving **Growth**". |
| **Motion** | Slow, floaty, confident — low gravity. Camera drifts; nothing snaps. |
| **Sound** | Warm synth pad, soft footsteps, a signature 3-note "X found" chime used in every video. Muted by default; starts on first tap. |

## 7. Production plan — top-level quality

### 7.1 Quality bars (what "global level" means, measurably)

| Bar | Target |
|---|---|
| First interaction | ≤ 5 s on 4G, inside Instagram / Facebook / TikTok in-app browsers |
| Frame rate | 60 fps on flagship, **30 fps floor on a RM800 Android**; adaptive quality tiers |
| Payload | ≤ 3 MB to first play; ≤ 10 MB per level, streamed |
| Stability | ≥ 99 % crash-free sessions; automatic 2D "Lite" fallback so nobody sees a blank screen |
| Scale | Load-tested to 10× forecast peak; leaderboard and registration survive an ad spike |
| Accuracy | Every booth within 0.5 m of the official plan; every name matched to the official list |
| Accessibility | BM + EN; readable at arm's length in a bright hall; reduced-motion option; one-thumb play |
| Craft benchmark | Side-by-side with Coastal World and Messenger without embarrassment |

### 7.2 Stack (unchanged from v1 research, confirmed)
**Three.js (WebGL2) + TypeScript + Vite** · React overlay for UI · Blender → glTF (Meshopt + KTX2) · world generated from booth JSON with instancing · **Cloudflare** Pages/Workers/R2 + Turnstile · **Supabase (Singapore)** Postgres · **Durable Objects** for presence · Claude API for "Ask X" · Meta CAPI + TikTok Events API server-side · Sentry.
Fallback engine: PlayCanvas. Not Unity / Godot / Unreal — they fail inside the in-app browsers your ad traffic arrives in.

### 7.3 Milestones — each one is releasable

| # | Milestone | Contents | Exit test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M0** | **Pre-production** | Data rebuild from PDF text layer (all 3 levels, phantom booths removed — 85 of the 466 entries in the Level 2 file are not real booths: they sit under the Wellness Corner, Kitchen, Photo Booth, Live Box and MIHAS 2027 pads, left over from a hidden PDF layer), join to official list. Art bible. Character sheets. Tech spike inside in-app browsers on 3 real phones. Legal: PDPA notice, T&Cs, organiser email. | One page of approved look; one phone running a 1,000-box scene at 30 fps in Instagram |
| **M1** | **Vertical slice** — *Level 2, Mission 01, end to end* | Level 2 world, one character, guide trail, registration, nexova card generation, Golden Ticket, staff scanner, analytics. Final-quality Launch Pad. | A stranger completes ad → play → register → card → scan with no help |
| **M2** | **Alpha — the full loop** | All four characters, BRAND/MARKET/SELL missions, leaderboard, "Bring your station online", admin panel, Lite mode, BM/EN. | 20 external testers; zero blockers |
| **M3** | **Beta — the full world** | Levels 1 and 3 in 3D, level-switching via escalators, presence, Ask X, sound, share cards, Wrapped. | Load test passed; device matrix passed |
| **M4** | **Gold + live ops** | Daily Drops, moderation tools, kill switches, on-call rota, booth screen mode. | Dress rehearsal at the booth |
| **M5** | **Afterlife** | 30-day open world, Wrapped, case-study film, organiser pitch, "any floor plan → game" product packaging. | — |

Whatever milestone is complete when the doors open is what ships; the rest lands as live updates. M1 alone already delivers the core promise — *find the X, get your card, come to the booth.*
